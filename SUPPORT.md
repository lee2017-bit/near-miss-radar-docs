# Support

Near-Miss Radar is maintained by an independent developer as a Codegeist 2026 entry.

- Bugs and questions: https://github.com/lee2017-bit/near-miss-radar-docs/issues
- Setup and demo steps: [SETUP.md](SETUP.md)
- Machine feed format: [EVENT_SCHEMA.md](EVENT_SCHEMA.md)

Near-Miss Radar is observation analytics. It does not control machines and is not a functional safety system. For an injury or immediate danger, follow your site's emergency procedure.
